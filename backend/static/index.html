Learn your textbook by answering, not reading.

Upload a PDF. A team of AI agents builds a topic-by-topic curriculum from it, asks you questions grounded in the text, scores your answers, and moves you forward only when you've really understood.

  1. Knowledge agent finds the right passages in your PDF with a FAISS vector search.
  2. Evaluator agent scores each answer 0–100 with Gemini 2.5 Flash.
  3. Tutor agent asks the next question, and moves to the next topic at 85%.
  4. Hint agent steps in with graduated hints when you're stuck.
